# How to reset your password

If you forgot your password, you can reset it from the login page.

1. Go to app.acmecloud.com/login and click "Forgot password?".
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Check your inbox for an email from support@acmecloud.com.
4. Click the reset link. Reset links expire after 30 minutes.
5. Choose a new password. It must be at least 12 characters and include one number.

If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder.
After 5 failed reset attempts, your account is locked for 1 hour.
