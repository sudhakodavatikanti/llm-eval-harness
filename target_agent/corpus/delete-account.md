# How to delete your account

Only the account owner can delete an account.

1. Go to Settings > Account > Delete account.
2. Type your workspace name to confirm.
3. Click "Delete permanently".

After you confirm, your account is deactivated right away.
Your data is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind. To restore it, email support@acmecloud.com within those 30 days.
After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Deleting your account cancels your subscription, but it does not refund your last payment automatically.
If you are still within the refund window, request a refund before you delete your account.

We recommend exporting your data before you delete your account.
