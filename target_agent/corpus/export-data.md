# How to export data

You can download a copy of all your workspace data at any time.

1. Go to Settings > Data > Export.
2. Choose a format: CSV or JSON. PDF exports are not supported.
3. Click "Start export".

Exports are prepared in the background and usually take up to 15 minutes.
When the export is ready, we email a download link to the workspace owner.
Download links expire after 7 days.

Each export can include up to 10 GB of data. Larger workspaces are split into multiple files.
Only workspace owners and admins can start an export.
You can run at most 3 exports per day.
